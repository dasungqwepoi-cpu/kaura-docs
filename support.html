Does K-AURA upload or save my face photo?

Never. Your camera frame is analyzed 100% on-device using Apple Vision to extract 10 geometric ratios and is immediately discarded in memory. Zero image bytes ever leave your iPhone.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

Toggle on "I don't know my exact birth time" during onboarding or when editing your profile. K-AURA automatically calculates a 6-character Three Pillars (Year, Month, Day) chart and Five Elements balance.

How do I edit my birth info without redoing my Face Scan?

Tap "Edit Profile" on the Aura tab, update your name, date, time, or birth city, and tap "Save & Keep Current Face Scan." You can also tap "Update Face Scan Too" whenever you want a fresh scan.

How do I delete all my data?

Scroll to the bottom of the Aura tab and tap "Reset My Data." Because K-AURA requires zero accounts and stores everything only on your device, resetting or uninstalling the app removes your data completely.
