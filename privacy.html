K-AURA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026 · Operator: K-AURA Studio · Contact: dasungqwepoi@gmail.com

1. Who We Are

K-AURA is an entertainment and self-reflection iOS app operated by K-AURA Studio. If you have any privacy questions or deletion requests, email us at dasungqwepoi@gmail.com.

2. Zero Photos Uploaded — 100% On-Device Face Analysis & Biometric Transparency

When you explicitly opt in to the Face Scan, K-AURA processes your camera frame strictly on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework to compute 10 non-identifying proportional balance ratios (such as facial thirds, eye spacing ratio, and jawline angle). Three-Layer Data Handling: (1) Raw photos and camera frames are never saved to disk, never uploaded to any server, never shared with third parties, and never used to train AI models. (2) Temporary facial landmark coordinates exist solely in your device's volatile memory (RAM) during capture and are discarded immediately upon completion of the ratio calculation. (3) The resulting 10 numerical balance ratios are stored locally on your device inside Apple SwiftData alongside your profile so you can revisit your reading without rescanning, and are permanently erased when you tap "Reset My Data" or uninstall K-AURA. K-AURA is designed strictly for entertainment and does not create, store, sell, lease, or share facial recognition templates or faceprints for identifying or authenticating individuals (including with respect to Illinois BIPA, Texas CUBI, and Washington biometric laws). You may skip the Face Scan at any time and use K-AURA with your birth chart alone.

3. Birth Information, Partner Inputs & Apple MapKit City Lookup

K-AURA uses the birth date, optional birth time, and birth city you enter to calculate your Korean Four Pillars (Saju) chart locally on your device. City search uses Apple MapKit to resolve city coordinates and time zones for True Solar Time calculation. K-AURA does not request GPS location tracking and does not require an account, email address, or phone number. If you enter another person's nickname or birth details in K-Chemistry, that data is computed locally on your iPhone, stored only in your local Recent Matches list if saved, and can be deleted individually or all at once at any time.

4. On-Device Report Generation & Data Collection

K-AURA v1.0 (Free Launch Edition) generates your Four Pillars birth chart, optional facial balance ratios, Daily Vibe Check, K-Chemistry compatibility scores, and personalized aura reading 100% on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data, birth details, or camera images. If optional cloud-enhanced readings or in-app purchases are introduced in a future update, this policy and our App Store privacy labels will be updated prior to release, and any optional feature will require your clear notice and consent.

5. Local Storage, Sharing & Deletion

Your optional nickname, birth inputs, optional 10 calculated facial balance ratios, derived aura report, and saved K-Chemistry match cards are stored locally on your device via Apple SwiftData and UserDefaults so you do not have to re-enter them. Shareable Aura Cards are rendered locally on your iPhone and only leave your device if you explicitly export or share them via the iOS share sheet. You can permanently delete all locally stored K-AURA data at any time by tapping "Reset My Data" inside the app or by uninstalling K-AURA.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Age Rules)

K-AURA is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or 16 and older where required in the EEA/UK) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable minimum age.

7. U.S. State (CCPA/CPRA) & European (EEA/UK GDPR) Privacy Rights

K-AURA does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (NSPrivacyTracking = false). Residents of California, other U.S. states with consumer privacy laws, the EEA, and the UK have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of personal information. Because K-AURA v1.0 stores data strictly locally on your iPhone, tapping "Reset My Data" or uninstalling the app immediately exercises your right to erasure. For any privacy inquiry, contact dasungqwepoi@gmail.com.

8. Entertainment Disclaimer

All Saju readings, facial geometry interpretations, Daily Vibe Checks, and K-Chemistry scores are provided for entertainment and self-reflection purposes only and do not constitute medical, psychological, financial, or relationship advice.
